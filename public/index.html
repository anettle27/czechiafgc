The challenge

The Olympics of robotics.

Every year, each country sends a single national team of high school students to design, build and program a robot, then compete against the rest of the world. It's the largest youth robotics event on the planet, and it moves to a new host country every year.

2026 season

Igniting Innovation.

This year's game is built around one of the planet's growing problems: wildfires.

Robots against wildfire

The 2026 theme, "Igniting Innovation", asks teams to build robots around wildfire prevention, mitigation and recovery. Every alliance plays the same game, so the difference comes down to design, strategy and teamwork.

We've spent the season engineering, building and programming our robot, and we're bringing it to Korea to see how it stacks up against the world.
